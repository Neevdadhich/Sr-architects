The Studio

A practice rooted in precision & warmth.

From our studio in Bharuch, we design modern homes that feel grounded in their place — tailored to family, climate, and craft. Our work moves fluidly between architecture, interiors, and turnkey execution, always with the same intent: spaces that look refined and live well.

Every project is led personally, drawn meticulously, and built with partners we trust. We believe luxury is in restraint — in proportions, materials, and the quiet confidence of a well-resolved detail.
